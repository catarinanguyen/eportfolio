Welcome,

My name is Catarina Nguyen, and I'm a student at Florida State University taking ENC 2135. When I started this class, I thought of genre as just the type of paper I was writing. Working on one topic all semester changed how I think about that.

Every major project I did this semester focused on overtreatment in cosmetic dentistry. In this portfolio you'll find my reflection on what I learned, my Investigative Field Essay after a global revision, and two supporting documents that show how I researched and planned my writing.
