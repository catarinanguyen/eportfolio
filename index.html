Welcome

My name is Catarina Nguyen, and I'm a student at Florida State University taking ENC 2135 with Professor Maria Hiers. This semester, all of my projects came back to one research question: what factors contribute to overtreatment in cosmetic dentistry.

My Investigative Field Essay looks at how social media, patient expectations, commercial pressure, and a dentist's own clinical judgment can lead to more treatment than a patient's teeth actually need. You can read my first draft of that essay now, with the revised version coming soon. The artifacts show the research and planning behind it. My reflection is posted as a draft for now, and the full 1,000-word version will be added later.
