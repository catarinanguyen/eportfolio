What this is

An outline I made in class while planning my Investigative Field Essay. It maps the introduction, research method, results, and discussion, plus a list of ideas I wanted to work in.

Skill it shows

My composing process. It shows my thinking before it was polished, including reminders to myself like finding empirical studies that connect social media to dental procedures.

Where it led

The outline ends on the idea that there is no single cause of overtreatment, only several factors working together. That gave the essay a direction to build toward from the start.
